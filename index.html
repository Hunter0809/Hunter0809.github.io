Research

Traffic Accident Scene Reconstruction with Three-Dimensional Gaussian Splatting

Ubiquitous Sensing and Spatial Intelligence · Academician Li Qingquan’s Group · May 2024–Present

As one of fifteen participants selected for the Academician-Led Science and Technology Innovation Training Camp, I joined the research group working on an aerial traffic-policing system for traffic accident investigation. My responsibility is the research and implementation of three-dimensional reconstruction algorithms for traffic accident scenes using three-dimensional Gaussian Splatting.

Vehicle glass, painted panels, and other highly reflective surfaces introduce optical distortions and missing reconstructed regions. To address this problem, I reviewed the relevant literature, participated in field-data collection, and implemented a specular-aware optimization method on collected datasets. The method integrates physical reflection priors and semantic constraints to suppress optical distortions and reduce incomplete reconstruction in reflective regions, improving the visual and geometric fidelity of the final scene.

Intelligent Ring Interaction and Authentication System for Extended Reality Devices

Provincial Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program for Undergraduates · May 2025–Present

I develop the device-side intelligent algorithms for an intelligent ring system that jointly supports gesture interaction and user authentication for extended reality devices. The objective is to preserve recognition quality while enabling lightweight deployment on constrained hardware.

My work includes designing an adaptive convolutional neural network, building a parameter-separation architecture that combines shared experts with task-specific towers, and developing a Siamese network with a contrastive-learning mechanism. These components are designed to handle gesture recognition and identity verification collaboratively rather than as unrelated tasks, while supporting efficient execution on the device.

Interpretable Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning and Compression for Large Language Models

Independent research under the supervision of Professor Hao Wang · Jun 2025–Present

I have conducted a systematic study of recent work on knowledge distillation and parameter-efficient fine-tuning for text-generating large language models. This work led me to focus on how model semantic concepts can be represented more explicitly while reducing the computational cost of adaptation and inference.

Under academic supervision, I designed an adapter architecture that represents model concept features through a discrete codebook. The design aims to improve interpretability and reduce end-to-end inference latency by decreasing the matrix operations used in conventional low-rank adaptation approaches. I continue to refine the architecture and evaluate its usefulness for lightweight deployment.

Inference Operator Optimization and Multimodal Model Compression

Model efficiency and deployment research · Sep 2025–Present

I began studying the Open Neural Network Exchange Runtime inference framework through the National College Student Computer System Capability Competition. My work includes investigating the execution and optimization of core operators such as attention, convolution, and batch normalization, with the goal of understanding practical inference bottlenecks and optimization opportunities.

In parallel, I developed an adaptive multimodal model compression approach guided by causal discovery. The approach uses causal relationships to inform pruning, quantization, and knowledge distillation decisions so that compression can preserve relationships that are important to model behavior.

Independent Projects

DouYun (豆韵)

Independent creator and full-stack developer · Live site · Source code

DouYun is an artificial-intelligence-assisted creative system that converts traditional Chinese cultural themes and user-supplied images into feasible fuse-bead designs. I independently designed and implemented the complete application so that a user can begin with a cultural theme, a local image, or a community work and move from creative intent to a manufacturable pattern rather than stopping at a generated illustration.

The workflow is organized as a controllable four-step pipeline: cultural-theme selection or image upload; subject identification with editable subject regions and colour composition; traditional-culture re-creation with user-editable generation prompts; and pattern production with an exportable grid, palette, material list, cultural explanation, and making plan. I implemented pixel sampling, palette mapping, grid rendering, product-form templates, and the image-to-pattern conversion logic required to make generated content usable in an actual handcraft process.

I built the system with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, server-side artificial-intelligence routes, browser-side image processing, and reusable data modules for cultural themes, product templates, aspect ratios, and colour mappings. Lightweight user settings and artificial-intelligence configuration are stored locally, while IndexedDB preserves project history and a community interface supports publication, browsing, search, and importing existing works for further editing. DouYun received the National First Prize, China Robot and Artificial Intelligence Competition (中国机器人及人工智能大赛).

Qixue Learn (启学智伴)

Independent creator and full-stack developer · Live site · Source code

Qixue Learn is a multi-agent personalized learning system for everyday student learning. I independently designed it around an “observe, diagnose, plan, execute, feedback, and optimize” loop: learning goals, task completion, weak knowledge points, mistakes, assessment outcomes, and resource-use behaviour are accumulated into a dynamic learner profile that informs the next recommendation, plan, and report.

The product connects intelligent question answering, photo-based problem search, homework and composition feedback, speaking practice, translation, document scanning, mistake analysis, staged quizzes, review plans, learning resources, and parent-facing reports. Its central workflows turn a submitted question or mistake into structured analysis, targeted explanation and practice; transform current goals, time availability, and mastery estimates into executable staged tasks; and feed subsequent outcomes back into the learner profile rather than treating each interaction as isolated.

I implemented the full-stack architecture with Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, and server-side application programming interfaces. A multi-agent orchestration layer separates profile analysis, task understanding, resource generation, planning, and effectiveness evaluation; schema validation constrains generated structured data before it reaches the learning workflow. The system combines client state and data fetching with persistent learning records, supports local and PostgreSQL-compatible storage, and uses optical character recognition, mathematical formula rendering, data visualization, and automated route, interface, and interaction tests. Qixue Learn has advanced to the national final round of the China Software Cup (中国软件杯); award results are pending.
