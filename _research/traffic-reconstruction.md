As one of fifteen participants selected for the Academician-Led Science and Technology Innovation Training Camp, I joined the research group working on an aerial traffic-policing system for accident investigation. I research and implement three-dimensional reconstruction algorithms for traffic accident scenes using Three-Dimensional Gaussian Splatting.

Highly reflective vehicle surfaces introduce optical distortions and missing regions. I reviewed the relevant literature, participated in field-data collection, and implemented a specular-aware optimization method that combines physical reflection priors and semantic constraints to reduce reflective-region incompleteness and improve visual and geometric fidelity.
