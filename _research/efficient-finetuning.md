I have conducted a systematic study of knowledge distillation and parameter-efficient fine-tuning for text-generating large language models. Under academic supervision, I designed an adapter architecture that represents model concept features through a discrete codebook.

The design aims to improve interpretability and reduce end-to-end inference latency by decreasing the matrix operations used in conventional low-rank adaptation approaches. I continue to refine the architecture and evaluate its use for lightweight deployment.
