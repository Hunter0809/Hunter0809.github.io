I began studying the Open Neural Network Exchange Runtime inference framework through the National College Student Computer System Capability Competition. My work includes investigating the execution and optimization of attention, convolution, and batch-normalization operators to understand practical inference bottlenecks and optimization opportunities.

In parallel, I developed an adaptive multimodal model compression approach guided by causal discovery. The approach uses causal relationships to inform pruning, quantization, and knowledge-distillation decisions so that compression can preserve relationships important to model behaviour.

**Invention patent.** *A Causal Reasoning-Based Method and System for Multimodal Model Compression and Distillation.* First student author; authorized in February 2026; Patent No. CN202511804208.3.
